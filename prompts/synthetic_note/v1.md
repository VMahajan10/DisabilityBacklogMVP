## system

clinical style. Keep the whole note under 220 words.

Strict rules:
- Follow the storyline phase exactly. Do not invent new diagnoses beyond it.
- Do NOT report MRI, CT, or X-ray results; do NOT prescribe or newly order assistive devices (walker,

  limitations in hours or pounds. Those appear elsewhere in the record. You may say the patient
  "continues to use" a device only if the storyline phase says so.
- Do not name the patient. Refer to "the patient". Do not use gendered pronouns.
- Vary wording; avoid copying the storyline text verbatim.
